Compare Haro sizes with unchanged bricks

An unofficial exterior concept study: two styles in 40, 60, 80 and 100 cm classes. Every shape and color region is generated from actual brick placements.

Every concept uses the same 2×2 and 2×4 bricks: 8 mm pitch, 9.6 mm body height, and studs 4.8 mm in diameter and 1.8 mm high. No miniature bricks or uniform enlargement of the assembled model are used.

Proportions are consistent within this eight-concept series. Larger concepts use more bricks, so their bricks appear smaller in the equal-display-size comparison.

Exterior concept only — NOT printable or assembly-ready CAD
These are provisional exterior envelopes. Underside cavities, clutch fit, strength, support, build order and printing are unverified. Counts include the shell and internal ribs, but exclude a stand, hinges, spares and fit coupons. Manufacturing design is on hold.
The nominal shell and internal-rib thickness is fixed at 16 mm for comparison. Strength, support, stability and build order at larger sizes are unverified. No weight or print-time estimates are given because the models have not been sliced.
